Dear Editors,

We submit "What Administrative Penalty Documents Measure: A Validation Audit of Food-Safety Enforcement Data in China" as an Original Research article.

Administrative penalty decisions are China's most abundant machine-readable food-safety enforcement record, yet studies that count them assume publication means the conduct occurred. We test that assumption on a frozen 875-document corpus and audit the food dimension such research depends on: the two annotation channels agree on 0.7460 of documents, but only 0.3852 of commodity matches concern the commodity the case is about.

We report which questions this record can answer and which it cannot, each tied to a product file and hash. The corpus index, code and products are public.

The manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere.

Sincerely,
Songze Zhu, on behalf of all authors
